How it works

  1. Export the rate sheet from your source system as a text PDF or Excel / CSV.
  2. Columns are matched by header name: Loc, Airline, Cd, scr, Crny, Min., Normal, +45 … +1000, FSC, SSC, Xray, Misc, CTG, Ams.
  3. Destination and currency are read from the Destination : and Currency : lines above the table.
  4. Each valid row replaces the older rate for the same airline, route and commodity, and is searchable immediately.
info Scanned or image-only PDFs can’t be read. Export a text PDF or a spreadsheet instead.
